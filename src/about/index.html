DAILYKIT

Five daily puzzles on one site. Each one gives every player in the world the same puzzle on the same day, and a new one arrives at your local midnight.

How a day works

  • Every game has its own puzzle number, counted in whole days from that game's opening date.
  • The day turns over at midnight where you are, not at midnight somewhere else.
  • Finishing any one game keeps your suite streak alive, so a busy day costs you nothing as long as you play something.

What is stored

Your boards, results, streaks, and settings are written to this browser's own storage and are never sent anywhere. There is no account to make and nothing to sign in to. Clearing this browser's site data clears your history with it, and a different browser or a different device starts over.

What is not here

  • No analytics, no third party scripts, and no cookies.
  • No advertising and nothing to buy.
  • No leaderboard and no accounts. The only thing you can send anyone is the result block you copy yourself, and it never reveals an answer.

Offline

After your first visit the site keeps itself and the next several days of puzzles on your device, so a lost connection does not cost you a day.
